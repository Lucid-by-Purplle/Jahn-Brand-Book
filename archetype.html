Archetype  ·  Two options under review

Two pairings.

Option A

Caregiver × Magician

How a mother makes all the problems go away like it never was really a thing. She translates warmth in the most magical sense. Care is felt in all its forms, there’s utmost reliance on this type to make the best of transformations happen without any fear, to get the best advice without doubting it. There’s always a hint of suspense / curiosity in the way they do it that you always want to learn and adapt in your ways. Warmth here doesn’t announce itself but changes the atmosphere of the room. There is magic without spectacle. Before and after so strong, you barely notice the crossing.

Option B

Everywoman × Outlaw

The primary archetype is everywoman, who feels like you, is always approachable, helps you become your best self, the feeling of home ‘a safe place.’

The secondary archetype here lets you reject what’s been imposed, doesn’t want you to do something just because you are asked to.

Two almost opposite energies working together to make life easy while having difficult conversations. A community that always welcomes you but you don’t have to earn it by behaving.

If I have to describe this archetype combo as a person - she is the nicest girl you know who can break the rules for you. She will say all the right things to make you feel included and comfortable but is strong enough to stand up for herself and you whenever required. She is the one who will stop you from apologizing when it’s not your fault. She will go to family functions, blend in and laugh but will also ask the elder uncle to stop talking to her sister like that. She will welcome you home, serve you great food and make you comfortable but won’t shy away from saying something that’s on her mind if you offend her. She will be friendly with everyone at work, make friends and hangout with a bunch of people but will always finish her point. She will not humiliate you but she will protect her dignity. She will be warm, loving and soft to her partner but will not let them disrespect her. Your friend who looks like you, talks like you, is always smiling and cracking jokes but won’t keep quiet she is not being taken seriously. She will fight the situations where someone or you are made to feel small.

For the most part this is a well behaved community that will not judge you or say something to make you feel out of place but will also be a rebel if it doesn’t agree with the rulebook. This community is kind to people but harsh when it comes to following rules/systems that seem unnecessary to them. The people here are relatable and have a familiar vibe but they aren’t always obedient. You will always feel safe and comfortable here but you will be pushed to make difficult decisions if you have to, but you won’t be alone, we will do it together.

You are made to feel safe and liked here, but also encouraged to be strong and hard when needed.

To look at it is a warm cozy house that looks like your family home, with the same kind of spoon and dishes you see at home with a hint of outlaw, a bold red, edgy painting in the corner of a room.

In the end the tone is always kind, words are never harsh, boundaries are always clear and the community is always together. This archetype will not be a complete rebel but will always maintain the boundaries, it’s for people who want to feel included but at the same time don’t want to lose their dignity. It will not tell you to be loud but will teach you to be clear. It will always be warm and welcoming for you to feel connected, but won’t let you get lost.

“My colors are soft, but my structure is firm”
