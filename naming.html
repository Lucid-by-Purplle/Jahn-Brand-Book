Naming

Why we retain the spelling.

Jahn. Our recommendation
vs
Janh. The alternative

We wanted to put down our thinking on why we should retain the spelling as Jahn.

For us, this is not just a spelling preference. The spelling changes the meaning, the emotional weight, and the way our brand is likely to be perceived.

Jahn. feels like a complete name. It’s intimate, emotional, and distinctive. It carries the feeling of the Hindi word jaan without spelling it in the most obvious way. It has warmth, love, closeness, and memory embedded in it. It also connects beautifully with the pillars we have been building the brand around, sisterhood, warmth, light, ritual, paradox, and transparency.

The fact that Janhvi had such an emotional response to the name also matters. This was not a reaction to a clever brand construct. It connected to something deeply personal for her. Her father used to call her mother jaan. That gives the name a real emotional anchor, which is very rare. Most celebrity beauty brands have to manufacture that kind of meaning after the fact. Here, the meaning already exists.

The period is also important. It gives the name a sense of pause and finality. It makes it feel more authored and intentional. It’s a statement of fact. You are Jahn.

Our concern with spelling it as Janh. is that it immediately makes the name feel smaller and more expected. It starts to read more directly as a shortened version of Janhvi’s name. That may make the celebrity connection more obvious, but it also risks making the brand easier to dismiss as another celebrity-led beauty brand.

That is not the space we are trying to build in.

We aren’t approaching this brand with the intent to simply borrow Janhvi’s fame. We are using her world, memories, taste, contradictions, and emotional truth to create something that can stand on its own.

Jahn. allows us to do that.

Janh. may feel more immediately connected to Janhvi, but it loses some of the emotional depth. It becomes more about recognition and that does her manifesto a disservice. Jahn. is about resonance, about connection, about leaving space for your story as well.

It’s the difference between We are with Janhvi and Janhvi’s with us.

We can build the Janhvi association through launch storytelling, campaign language, PR, social content, and the way she introduces the brand. But if we dilute the name itself, we lose something that will be very hard to recover later.

So our recommendation is that we retain Jahn.

It is more distinctive, more emotional, more universal, and more aligned with the kind of brand we are trying to create.
