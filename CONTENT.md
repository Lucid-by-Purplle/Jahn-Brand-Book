# Jahn. Brand Book — content + build spec

Multi-page website, nav across top. Shared: styles.css, app.js.
Brand mark: diamond/heart motif. Wordmark "Jahn." with red period.

## Pages & nav order
1. index.html        — Cover / landing
2. manifesto.html    — JK's Manifesto (her original words)
3. interpretation.html — Our interpretation (3 principles + transparency signature)
4. archetype.html    — Caregiver x Magician
5. directions.html   — Two finalised design directions: Krystal Bloom + IndiPop
6. naming.html       — Jahn. vs Janh. naming logic

## Source material lives in this repo:
- /docs  — paste the manifesto, interpretation, naming PDFs/text here
- /assets/krystal  — Krystal Bloom product shots (crystal-faceted, prismatic)
- /assets/indipop  — IndiPop product shots (silver chrome, scalloped labels)

## Design notes
- Krystal Bloom = prismatic, crystal-cut, magical, rainbow light. Cool pastels.
- IndiPop = clean silver chrome bodies, white scalloped labels, soft retro-Indian charm, dusty rose cartons.
- Both carry Jahn. wordmark + diamond-heart motif.
- Archetype: Caregiver x Magician — warmth that changes the room, magic without spectacle.
- Naming: retain "Jahn." (resonance, jaan, emotional anchor) over "Janh." (too literally Janhvi).
